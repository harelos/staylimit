Privacy Policy

Overstay — know your days before they run out · Last updated July 22, 2026

Overstay is built by TigerBrands Global to be private by default. It works fully offline, needs no account, shows no ads, and keeps every trip you log on your own phone.

The short version

  • We do not collect, upload, sell or share your personal data.
  • Every zone and trip you log stays in local storage on your device.
  • No accounts, no logins, no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking.

What Overstay stores

Overstay stores the zones you create (name and day-limit rule) and the trips you log (entry and exit dates) directly on your device. This information never leaves your phone through Overstay. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

Not immigration, tax or legal advice

Overstay is a personal record-keeping and arithmetic tool. It is not immigration, tax, or legal advice, and it does not verify your status with any government or authority. Day-limit rules vary by country, change over time, and can have exceptions Overstay does not know about. Always confirm your situation against official sources before making travel or residency decisions.

Purchases

Overstay offers an optional paid subscription for tracking additional zones. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We never see or store your card number or billing details — we only receive confirmation from Google Play that an entitlement is active so the app can unlock its paid features.

Data sharing

We do not share your information with anyone, because we do not collect it. The only outbound connection Overstay makes is to Google Play for purchases. No advertising, marketing or analytics services are used.

Children's privacy

Overstay is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Because your data is stored locally and not transmitted to us, it stays under your control and your device's own protections.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the revised version at this same address.
